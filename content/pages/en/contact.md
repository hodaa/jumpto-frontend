Questions, bug reports, and feedback are all welcome — they are the main reason this page exists.

## Email

The surest way to reach us: [**support@qfza.app**](mailto:support@qfza.app). We usually reply within a day or two.

## What to include

A message goes much faster with a couple of specifics:

- **A bug or an empty result?** The YouTube link, the phrase you searched, and whether captions are on for that video. If captions were off, that alone explains the empty result.
- **A transcription that looked wrong?** The timestamp and the line, so we can see what happened.
- **Something about the service?** A sentence is plenty.

Please do not send links to private or paywalled content.

## Before you write

The [FAQ](/faq/) already answers what Qfza searches, which videos work, and why a search sometimes comes back empty — it may save you the round trip.

## Not affiliated with YouTube

Qfza is an independent tool. We cannot help with anything to do with a YouTube account, a video's copyright status, or the accuracy of YouTube's own captions. For those, [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/) is the right place.
